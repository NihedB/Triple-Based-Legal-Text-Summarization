You will be given a news article. You will then be given one summary written for this article.

Your task is to rate the summary on one metric.

Please make sure you read and understand these instructions carefully. Please keep this document open while reviewing, and refer to it as needed.

Evaluation Criteria:

Consistency (1-5) - the factual alignment between the summary and general world knowledge. A factually consistent summary contains only statements that are consistent with established real-world facts. Annotators were also asked to penalize summaries that contained hallucinated facts.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Read the summary carefully and identify the factual claims it presents.
2. Check whether each claim in the summary is factually correct according to general world knowledge, rather than the source article.
3. Assign a score for consistency based on the Evaluation Criteria.
